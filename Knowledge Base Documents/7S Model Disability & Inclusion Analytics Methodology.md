# Disability & Inclusion Analytics Methodology

## Using the McKinsey 7S Model

This document reverse engineers a practical methodology from corporate
disability inclusion commitments to enable analytics departments to
align strategy, culture, and systems using the McKinsey 7S Framework.

The approach positions analytics as a **strategic value engine** that
converts inclusion commitments into measurable business outcomes such as
growth, engagement, retention, innovation, and brand trust.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Step 1: Start with Shared Values (The Centre)

## Objective

Define the analytics department's purpose as enabling equitable
experiences and inclusive growth, not just producing reports.

## Instructions

1.  Define a departmental mission statement.
2.  Establish guiding principles (inclusion is measurable, accessibility
    drives innovation, etc.).
3.  Align metrics with values (representation, accessibility outcomes,
    engagement).
4.  Create a "Data for Inclusion Charter" covering ethics and privacy.

## Suggested Prompts

-   What business problems related to accessibility are we trying to
    solve?
-   Where might inaccessible experiences be creating hidden revenue
    loss?
-   Are we prioritising speed of insight or perfection of reporting?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Step 2: Define the Strategy (Hard S)

## Objective

Position disability and inclusion analytics as a competitive advantage.

## Instructions

1.  Define 12--24 month objectives (engagement increases, retention
    improvements, usability gains).
2.  Decide strategic positioning (cost avoidance, growth, innovation,
    workforce performance).
3.  Create an analytics use-case portfolio.
4.  Define value measurement (revenue, cost reduction, engagement
    metrics).

## Suggested Prompts

-   Where are we losing customers due to inaccessible experiences?
-   Which improvements produce measurable ROI?
-   Are we positioned as a reporting function or a growth partner?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Step 3: Align the Structure (Hard S)

## Objective

Ensure organisational design enables analytics to influence decisions.

## Instructions

1.  Select an operating model (centralised, decentralised, hybrid).
2.  Define decision rights.
3.  Establish cross-functional governance.
4.  Create an Inclusion Insights Council.

## Suggested Prompts

-   Who acts on this data once we produce it?
-   Which departments need embedded analysts?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Step 4: Audit the Systems (Hard S)

## Objective

Evaluate whether infrastructure supports inclusion analytics.

## Instructions

1.  Audit data sources (HR, customer platforms, accessibility tools).
2.  Evaluate data gaps (self-identification, assistive technology
    usage).
3.  Improve workflows.
4.  Implement dashboards.

## Suggested Prompts

-   What data do we wish we had but don't?
-   Where are manual processes slowing insight delivery?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Step 5: Evaluate Staff and Skills (Soft S)

## Objective

Ensure talent capability matches strategic ambition.

## Instructions

1.  Identify role types (engineers, scientists, translators, UX
    researchers).
2.  Conduct skills mapping.
3.  Build inclusion literacy.
4.  Recruit diverse talent.

## Suggested Prompts

-   Do we understand user experience, not just data?
-   What new skills will AI require in two years?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Step 6: Define Leadership Style (Soft S)

## Objective

Shift toward experimental, insight-driven leadership.

## Instructions

1.  Encourage curiosity and psychological safety.
2.  Introduce rituals (insight demos, experiment reviews).
3.  Model data-driven behaviour at leadership level.

## Suggested Prompts

-   What decision changed because of this data?
-   What experiment should we run next?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Step 7: Identify Gaps and Iterate

## Objective

Maintain alignment across all seven elements.

## Instructions

1.  Conduct quarterly 7S alignment reviews.
2.  Perform maturity scoring.
3.  Identify priority interventions.
4.  Maintain a continuous improvement loop.

## Suggested Prompts

-   Where are we over-ambitious compared to capability?
-   Which gaps create the biggest risk?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Measurement Framework

Inputs → Activities → Outputs → Outcomes → Impact

Example: Training → Accessible product design → Improved usability →
Higher engagement → Revenue growth

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Executive Summary

Disability and inclusion analytics represents a significant opportunity
for organisations to unlock growth, innovation, and brand trust.
Accessibility improvements expand market reach by enabling participation
from disabled people, ageing populations, and individuals experiencing
temporary or situational impairments --- meaning the benefits extend to
the entire customer base. When analytics translates inclusion
commitments into measurable outcomes, organisations can identify hidden
friction points that reduce engagement, conversion, and loyalty.
Inclusive experiences also strengthen brand perception by signalling
empathy, modernity, and social responsibility, which increasingly
influence purchasing and employment decisions. Workforce analytics
further drives value by improving retention, productivity, and talent
attraction. Organisations that embed accessibility insights into product
design often discover unmet needs that lead to innovation and new
revenue streams. Aligning analytics capability with inclusion strategy
moves accessibility from compliance to a strategic growth lever.
Ultimately, inclusive design improves experiences for everyone,
increasing engagement across the full audience spectrum and
strengthening long‑term business performance.
